About

BLAST is a conference series focusing on Boolean algebras, Lattices, Algebraic logic and universal algebra, Set theory, and set-theoretic and point-free Topology. Since its first meeting at the University of Denver in 2008, the conference has rotated among universities across the United States. (BLAST series home page)

BLAST 2027, the 17th meeting in the series, will be hosted by the Department of Mathematics at the University of Notre Dame from Saturday, March 6 through Tuesday, March 9, 2027. Reflecting the breadth of the logic group at Notre Dame, this year's program will also feature model theory and computability theory alongside the traditional BLAST areas.

The meeting will consist of tutorials, plenary lectures, and contributed talks. Graduate students and early-career researchers are especially encouraged to attend and to speak.

Registration

All participants are asked to register by February 1, 2027. To register, please fill out the registration form.

A modest registration fee helps cover coffee breaks:

  • $80 for tenure-track faculty and above
  • $25 for postdocs
  • Free for graduate and undergraduate students

Financial support

Thanks to support from the National Science Foundation, limited funds are available to help cover travel and lodging for participants. Priority will be given to graduate students, postdocs, and other early-career researchers.

Requests for travel support are due by February 1, 2027. Support will be awarded on a rolling basis, so we encourage you to send your request as early as possible. To request support, answer "Yes" to the travel support question on the registration form.

Graduate students requesting support should also ask their advisor to send a brief endorsement by email to both organizers, Natasha Dobrinen (ndobrine@nd.edu) and Nick Ramsey (sramsey5@nd.edu).

NSF travel support for the BLAST series is administered by the Department of Mathematics at the University of Colorado Boulder, which will process reimbursements.
